1.1 PROJECT INTRODUCTION

Background, Motivation & Problem Definition

📌 1. Background
  • Traditional motor insurance claim processing relies on manual paper-based field inspections.
  • Assessors physically examine vehicle damage, record body panels on clipboards, and type data into legacy branch systems.
  • Leads to extended claim processing cycles ranging from 2 to 5 business days per claim.
🚀 2. Motivation
  • Rapid growth in vehicle ownership has exponentially increased daily claim submission volumes.
  • Paper forms lead to lost historical records, unindexed photo folders, and administrative overheads.
  • Need for an automated visual AI + ERP platform to standardize inspections and shorten payouts.
⚠️ 3. Problem Definition
  • Subjective Cost Evaluation: Different assessors assign unequal repair prices to identical damage scuffs.
  • False Positive Detections: Off-the-shelf CNNs misidentify background fences, shadows, and walls as car damage.
  • Fragmented Portals: Legacy portals lack secure role-based portals for Admins, Assessors, and Customers.
1.2 OBJECTIVES AND GOALS

Primary Goal, Solution Architecture & Outcomes

🎯 Primary & Technical Objectives

Primary Goal: Develop and evaluate a web-based role-based ERP information system integrating deep learning instance segmentation for automated motor claim assessment.

  • Fine-Tuned YOLOv8 Nano: Train a 7-class instance segmentation model (best.pt) for scratch, dent, tear, missing part, broken lamp, puncture, and broken glass.
  • Dual-Model Spatial Gate: Couple COCO vehicle detector (yolov8n.pt) with a 20% IoU spatial overlap rule to suppress background false positives.
  • Decoupled ERP Architecture: Build Next.js 14 frontend, FastAPI Python backend, and 15-table MySQL database (Vehicle_Analyzis).
✨ Addressing the Problem & Expected Outcomes
  • Real-Time Visual Analysis: Performs damage localization, boundary mask rendering, and cost calculation in < 2 seconds.
  • Standardized Financial Costing: Auto-calculates repair totals with itemized line items, 15% VAT, and policy deductible subtractions.
  • Automated PDF Reports: Generates downloadable official claim PDF reports containing visual overlays and company branding.
  • Multi-Role Security: Enforces Argon2id password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies, and 15-table audit logging.

1.4 KEY ACHIEVEMENTS

Empirical Results, Spatial Gating & Features

📊 Model Performance

Achieved at Epoch 44 (Best recorded validation checkpoint):

  • Box Precision: 55.91%
  • Box mAP50: 44.80%
  • Mask Precision: 53.78%
  • Mask mAP50: 41.20%

Strongest classes: Broken Glass (78.8% mAP) & Missing Part (63.6% mAP).

🛡️ 20% IoU Spatial Gate

Innovative dual-model validation rule:

  • COCO vehicle detector establishes car boundary.
  • Damage box accepted only if IoU $\ge$ 20% with car.
  • Suppresses 78% of off-target background false positives.
  • Operator close-up toggle for tight panel scuffs.
⚙️ Enterprise ERP Engine
  • Multi-Role RBAC: Dedicated portals for Admins, Assessors, and Customers.
  • Argon2id Security: Password hashing & HttpOnly session cookies.
  • PDF & Audit Logs: Instant PDF report generation + 15-table MySQL audit logging.
1.5 CHALLENGES AND SOLUTIONS

Engineering Challenges, Overcoming Bottlenecks & Lessons

🚨 Challenge 1: CPU Training Latency

Problem: Training on CPU required ~96 minutes per epoch (~24h for 15 epochs).

Solution: Engineered a multi-stage CPU-to-GPU resume workflow:

  • Transferred `last.pt` checkpoint to NVIDIA RTX workstation (CUDA 12.8).
  • Executed `prepare_dataset.py` to re-anchor absolute local paths in `prepared_dataset.yaml`.
  • Resumed at Epoch 16 (`YOLO('last.pt').train(resume=True)`), dropping epoch time from 96m to 1m 18s.
⚡ Challenge 2: Background Clutter & Session State

Problem: Background fences/shadows caused false alarms; Streamlit prototype suffered from widget state loss and page reruns.

Solution & Lessons Learned:

  • Designed dual-model 20% IoU spatial gating rule to filter non-vehicle regions.
  • Migrated presentation layer to decoupled Next.js 14 App Router + FastAPI REST backend.
  • Enforced mandatory human operator review to ensure financial accuracy.
1.6 LIMITATIONS AND FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS

System Scope Boundaries & Phase 2 Roadmap

⚠️ Current System Scope Limitations
  • 2D Visual Inspection Bounded: System evaluates 2D surface photographs. Internal engine diagnostics, mechanical teardowns, and chassis frame alignments fall outside scope.
  • No Direct Bank Payout API: Calculates financial repair costs, 15% VAT, and deductibles, but does not execute direct online bank money transfers.
  • Mask Polygon Editing: Displays AI-generated masks; interactive polygon redrawing is deferred to Phase 2.
🚀 Phase 2 Development Roadmap
  • Negative Background Dataset: Train on explicit empty background images with empty label files to further reduce false alarms.
  • 18-Part Body Panel Segmenter: Train a dedicated 18-part panel validator (door, fender, bumper, hood).
  • Held-Out Untouched Test Set: Evaluate new models against a strictly isolated test set.
  • Mobile Inspection App: Develop native iOS/Android apps with live video damage detection streams.
